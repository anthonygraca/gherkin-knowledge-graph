# AI-03 — Revision-aware retrieval snapshot and cache lifecycle

**Issue:** [#32](https://github.com/anthonygraca/gherkin-knowledge-graph/issues/32) · **Parent:** F-08
[#14] · **Milestone:** M3 · **Owner:** A; B/C review manifests/provenance.
**Canonical criteria:** [AI-03.feature](AI-03.feature) (refinement draft, 6 scenarios).
**Status:** needs owner adoption of the proposed re-scope in ADR 0012. The original CTA-owned
embedding-refresh obligation is superseded only if the owner records that disposition.

## Scope (proposed)

CTA-owned content-hash change planning, corpus cache keys, model/configuration manifests, isolated
snapshot publication, compatibility checks, and refresh reporting. CocoIndex owns chunking/embeddings/
index storage; CTA does not claim internal embedding counts.

## Non-goals

A CTA source-code chunker or vector store; claims about unobserved CocoIndex vector reuse; semantic
indexing of requirements/scenarios; changing M2 freshness policy.

## Verification plan (planned, not executed)

Hand-labeled baseline change sets; evidence-only commits; model/configuration changes; additions and
deletions; failed publication leaves previous snapshot usable; mixed snapshot candidates rejected;
items_embedded reported `not_available` with availability marker. Deterministic adapters make most
criteria model-free.
