# REQ-001 — Instant withdrawal availability

Customers must be able to withdraw funds from an account immediately whenever the account
holds sufficient funds. Withdrawals are requested in cents and are recorded in the account
history so the customer can see what happened.

## Acceptance criteria

- A withdrawal debits the account balance by exactly the requested amount.
- A withdrawal larger than the balance is rejected and leaves the balance unchanged.
- Amounts that are not whole dollars are permitted (for example 10.50 from a 100.00 balance).
- Every successful withdrawal appears in the account history.
- Zero and negative amounts are rejected.
