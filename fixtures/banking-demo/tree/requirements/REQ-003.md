# REQ-003 — Daily transfer limit boundary semantics (superseded)

A transfer that brings the day's transferred total to exactly the daily limit is permitted;
only a transfer that would exceed the limit is rejected.

Superseded on 2026-09-20 by REQ-002, which restates the policy in full. The requirement and
its verification chain are retained for audit history.
