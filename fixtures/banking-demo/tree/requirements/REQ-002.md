# REQ-002 — Daily transfer limit enforcement

Each account holder has a daily transfer allowance of 1,000.00. The ledger must reject any
transfer that would push the day's transferred total above the allowance and must not record
the rejected transfer. REQ-002 restates and supersedes the boundary wording of REQ-003.

## Acceptance criteria

- A transfer that keeps the daily total at or below 1,000.00 is recorded.
- A transfer that would exceed the allowance is rejected with a daily limit error.
- A rejected transfer leaves the daily total and the ledger entries unchanged.
